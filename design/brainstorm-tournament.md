## Givens (from the docs)

- **When and where:** the midpoint, after gym 4, in Hanashiro's castle grounds (a castle town of
  apples and cherry trees; Mamoru, gym 4's gardener, survives the disaster, shaken).
- **Story-outline Act 2:** Nami and Yuki are competitors. The villains move during the event. The
  disaster kills Fuyumi and Hashimoto. He learns Nami is with the villains.
- **The disaster** is villain-caused; the League plays Fuyumi's death down afterwards, and a few
  towns later (Act 3) Akira joins Minato openly.
- **The villains:** the Eien Survey's true name, Tokoyo (常世), comes out here. Minato (leader,
  lives); Hashimoto (Nami's mentor, dies here); Hinata returns, clearly on the other side; the
  Route 4 Survey member is seen near where they move (the one who reports Celebi to Minato).
  Their goal: draw energy from other worlds through Palkia's door to sustain Arceus.
- **People with tournament beats:** Akira (his evolved starter first shows here; his practice and
  training steps close here); Yuki (step 1: a spar before her match, her answer after); Fuyumi
  (her chain closes here; step 2's promise to look out for Akira comes back); Haru (step 2 is
  "before the tournament"); KAITO (League-sponsored, heading for it); Ren (a League trainer; his
  step 1 is "after the tournament").
- **Tone:** deaths shown plainly and briefly, no deathbed speeches; villains believe they're
  right and lose gracefully (`dialogue-style.md`).
- **Levels:** gym 4's ace Lv 32; Act 3 starts with gym 5.
