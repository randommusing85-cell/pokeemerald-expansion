Working notes. Decisions move into `story-outline.md`, `characters.md`, `side-quests.md`,

## Givens (from the docs)

- **Story-outline Act 4:** (1) the Pokémon League: partway through the Elite Four, Minato's plan
  starts; (2) Seiji, the Champion, admits the cover-up and joins him; (3) the door mountain: on the
  way up Kaede fights beside him in a tag battle against two of Minato's people (Tetsu and Ren hold
  off others if their points are high); with Seiji, Haru and Yuki he fights Minato and Akira;
  Celebi dies protecting him; (4) he helps the cycle finish safely; Arceus renews; the journal's
  pages go blank; Kaede sees the tale happen; (5) he says goodbye to his friends; Palkia sends him
  home. Ending: a hospital in our world; a glimpse of a woman who looks exactly like Nami (a small
  Lapras charm hint). Epilogue: the memory post-game.
- **The League** is on the door mountain's peninsula, a white building on the far shore of the
  caldera lake, the door mountain beside it (seen from Kawara). The Bell Badge (gym 8) opens it.
- **Minato:** leads Tokoyo; believes he can stop the cycle by drawing energy from other worlds
  (the hero's included) through Palkia's door; calm, unapologetic; lives. Tokoyo opened a thinning
  at the tournament to test a small machine; at the door mountain he builds the full machine.
- **Akira** joined Minato; fights against the hero at the end; his points change his last scene
  and epilogue flash, not his choice. **Nami** chose her world; "they choose each other anyway";
  the core romance scenes always play. **Seiji** ordered the cover-up. **Celebi** came from a
  future where the world ended; it can't fight or jump again.
- **Her notes (the professor's):** an outsider has to carry the change (and forgets it); Arceus has
  to be let go, not saved: help the cycle finish instead of feeding it, the opposite of Minato.
- **Friends:** all survive; he says goodbye to each on-screen; keepsakes (Haru's finished map,
  Yuki's festival cord, Akira's object of Fuyumi's, Nami's Lapras charm hint).
- **Systems:** the thinning effect (built), the thin-place battle effect, the follower partner for
  tag battles, relationship points, Kumo for fast travel. Gym 8's ace Lv 55.

## Decided

- **The plan starts (r1 1a):** the door opens: a thinning spreads from the mountain across the
  caldera into the League mid-run; the screen drains, the battle hall goes grey, the Elite Four stop.
  (Akira can still arrive in it.)
- **The Elite Four (r1 2b):** three new members, and Ibuki as the fourth: he returns to the League
  he walked away from because it needs him now. The other three kept simple.
- **When the run breaks (r1 3b):** after the fourth, at Seiji's door: the Champion's battle becomes
  his confession. He never becomes Champion.
- **Nami (r1 4a):** on the mountain, on Minato's side: their last battle on the way up. She never
  changes her mind; after the battle she's still there.
- **The final battles (r1 5b):** in a row: the hero against Akira one on one (the rival's ending),
  then the hero and Seiji against Minato (the Champion's turn). Haru and Yuki are there (holding
  Tokoyo's people back; r2).
- **Celebi (r1 6a):** Minato's machine fires to pull the hero (an outsider, the bridge between
  worlds) through the door, and Celebi takes it. Plain, physical, no speech.
- **The climb (r2 1a):** the caldera lake path → Kaede's tag battle against two Tokoyo members at
  the riverbed of souls → Tetsu's path and Ren holding the side trail (if their points are high) →
  Nami's battle near the summit → the top: Akira, then Minato. Haru and Yuki go up with Seiji and
  stand at the top.
- **After Minato (r2 2a):** Palkia appears at the door. He walks into the light; the cycle finishes
  through him; Arceus renews (felt, not shown: the colour coming back); his memories of Eien start
  to go.
- **Minato (r2 3b):** lives and walks away, unrepentant, in silence.
- **The goodbyes (r2 4b):** a short walk through Eien: Kumo flies him to each friend's place in turn
  (Haru's house, Minori, Kaede's shrine, Nami's coast...), then back to the door.
- **Nami's goodbye (r2 5b):** at Lake Usurai, where they met: frozen, quiet. She lends him Lapras to
  cross one last time.
- **The Elite Four's other three (r2 6a):** Dragon, Dark and Steel (Ibuki's Fighting beats Dark and
  Steel).
