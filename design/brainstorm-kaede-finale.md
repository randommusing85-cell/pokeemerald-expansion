# Brainstorm: Kaede's finale battle and team

Running notes. Nothing is decided until it's under **Decided**; decisions move into
`characters.md`, `side-quests.md` and `story-outline.md` when the user agrees.

## Givens

- At the door mountain Kaede always comes to help; with enough points Tetsu and Ren come
  too, "each holding off part of Minato's forces in their own battle" (`side-quests.md`).
- The main fight: with Seiji, Haru and Yuki he fights Minato and Akira; Celebi dies
  protecting him (`story-outline.md`, Act 4 beat 3).
- Her team: an Eien Mightyena (the shrine's guardian) plus Ghost types, species
  `TODO(design)` (`characters.md`).
- The mountain is a thin place: its terrain boosts Ghost and Psychic moves (exact rules
  `TODO(design)`, `game-bible.md`).
- Friends fight beside him in scripted tag battles; which friends get them is `TODO(design)`.
  The engine supports partner battles (`src/data/battle_partners.party`, like Steven's).
- Villains are principled and lose gracefully (`dialogue-style.md`).
- Eien variants: 9 starter variants plus 5-10 others for the whole game (`variants.md`).
- Fan knowledge: he knows Gen 1-5; later species and Eien variants are "???".
- She has a battle sprite (`TRAINER_PIC_EIEN_KAEDE`); a back sprite exists only for the hero.

## Decided

- **Form (1a):** a tag battle beside him: the hero and Kaede against two of Minato's people,
  with the engine's partner battles. She needs a back sprite.
- **Opponents (2a):** two ordinary members of Minato's group: nameless, principled, gracious
  when they lose.
- **Team theme (3a):** Eien Mightyena plus Gen 1-5 Ghost types that fit snow and shrine
  folklore (he recognizes them). No new Eien variants.
- **Size (4a):** three Pokémon, the usual per side in a tag battle, at finale level.
- **Terrain (5a):** the thin-place terrain is active, as on the whole mountain; her Ghosts
  get its boost. Depends on the terrain's rules (`TODO(design)`).
- **Points (6a):** the battle is always the same; her points change only her lines.
