# Brainstorm: the route north of the lake (Route 2, placeholder)

Working notes for the route past Shimotsuki's lake path (`MAP_EIEN_ROUTE2`, a placeholder
name). Decisions move into a location doc when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- The lake path north of Shimotsuki is built; Nami is met there (end of milestone 1). The
  shimotsuki doc says it "becomes the start of the next route later".
- Act 1 beats 7-8 (`story-outline.md`): the villains appear (principled, gracious when
  beaten); signs of the fading (small disasters, off-screen losses); he battles Nami; gyms
  2-4. Gym leaders 2-8 are undecided.
- Yuki's cameo is "a route after gym 2"; Ren is a League trainer at gym 2 or 3
  (`side-quests.md`). Kaede's first real meeting and Fuyumi's home rematch happen in
  Shimotsuki in milestone 2.
- Kaede's tablet quest wants "one tablet per few maps".
- Region: Tohoku / Aomori snow country and coast; a volcanic mountain later (the door
  mountain). About 5-10 Eien variants in the whole game; Poochyena is the first.
- Level curve past gym 1 is open (gym 1 ace Lv 14; starters evolve about Lv 18).

## Decided

- **Destination (r1 1a):** a mountain pass up to a hot-spring town, gym 2's town. Warmth after
  the Ice gym; the steam vents foreshadow the volcanic door mountain.
- **Villains' first appearance (r1 2c):** helping after the route's small disaster; he only
  learns later who they were.
- **The first small disaster (r1 3c+b):** a patch where the world thins: the snow stops
  falling, colours drain, Celebi reacts; Pokémon flee it.
- **Nami's battle (r1 4c):** later, between gyms 3 and 4. Not on this route.
- **Shape (r1 5a+d):** one long route (the lake path is its south end) plus a small optional
  roadside shrine with a tablet for Kaede's quest.
- **Variant (r1 6d):** no new variant on this route; the next Eien variant answers gym 2, the
  way Poochyena answered gym 1.
