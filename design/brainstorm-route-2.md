# Brainstorm: the route north of the lake (Route 2, placeholder)

Working notes for the route past Shimotsuki's lake path (`MAP_EIEN_ROUTE2`, a placeholder
name). Decisions move into a location doc when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- The lake path north of Shimotsuki is built; Nami is met there (end of milestone 1). The
  shimotsuki doc says it "becomes the start of the next route later".
- Act 1 beats 7-8 (`story-outline.md`): the villains appear (principled, gracious when
  beaten); signs of the fading (small disasters, off-screen losses); he battles Nami; gyms
  2-4. Gym leaders 2-8 are undecided.
- Yuki's cameo is "a route after gym 2"; Ren is a League trainer at gym 2 or 3
  (`side-quests.md`). Kaede's first real meeting and Fuyumi's home rematch happen in
  Shimotsuki in milestone 2.
- Kaede's tablet quest wants "one tablet per few maps".
- Region: Tohoku / Aomori snow country and coast; a volcanic mountain later (the door
  mountain). About 5-10 Eien variants in the whole game; Poochyena is the first.
- Level curve past gym 1 is open (gym 1 ace Lv 14; starters evolve about Lv 18).

## Decided

- **Destination (r1 1a):** a mountain pass up to a hot-spring town, gym 2's town. Warmth after
  the Ice gym; the steam vents foreshadow the volcanic door mountain.
- **Villains' first appearance (r1 2c):** helping after the route's small disaster; he only
  learns later who they were.
- **The first small disaster (r1 3c+b):** a patch where the world thins: the snow stops
  falling, colours drain, Celebi reacts; Pokémon flee it.
- **Nami's battle (r1 4c):** later, between gyms 3 and 4. Not on this route.
- **Shape (r1 5a+d):** one long route (the lake path is its south end) plus a small optional
  roadside shrine with a tablet for Kaede's quest.
- **Variant (r1 6d, r2 6a):** the next Eien variant answers gym 2, the way Poochyena answered
  gym 1. It lives on this route, in grass near the town end: the route's one notable Pokémon.
- **Gym 2 (r2 1b):** Fighting. The leader is a mountain ascetic who trains under the hot
  waterfalls (Tohoku's yamabushi). Fighting threatens Eien Poochyena (Rock/Dark), which gives
  the new variant its job.
- **Villains at the patch (r2 2c+d):** they rope the patch off and warn people away, as if
  they've seen it before; one member stays behind and chats with him, friendly and curious
  about Celebi (a first named member; placeholder name).
- **Nami (r2 3a):** seen far off crossing the lake on Lapras. No words.
- **The patch (r2 4a):** midway, on the main path; an unavoidable scene as he passes, then he
  walks around it.
- **The variant (r3 1a):** Eien Hoothoot, Ghost/Flying: a lantern owl of the night pass.
  Immune to Fighting moves, hits Fighting with Flying, echoes the thin places. Gets a research
  entry like Poochyena's.
- **Gym 2 leader (r3 2c):** a former League fighter who left the League and came to the falls;
  quietly sceptical of it (a seed for the cover-up, not spelled out). Name open.
- **Faction look (r3 3b, placeholder):** field gear: headlamps, measuring poles, notebooks.
  They look like researchers, which is why nobody finds them suspicious.
- **Friendly member (r3 4a+c):** a young field researcher who loves Pokémon and is fascinated
  by Celebi; warm and open. Returns at the tournament, clearly on the other side. Name open.
- **Route trainers (r3 5c):** a mix of locals at their jobs (woodcutter, inn porter, charcoal
  burner) and pilgrims climbing to the falls (Fighting-leaning, previewing the gym).
- **Haru (r3 6a):** catches up on the route for his map notes and gives the Town Map, closing
  his step 1. Akira's next battle can come at gym 2.
- **Roadside tablet (r2 5a):** about thin places ("where the snow stops, the gate is near" is
  the direction, not the text). The text is still to write.
