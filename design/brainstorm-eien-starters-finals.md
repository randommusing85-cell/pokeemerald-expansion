# Brainstorm: the Eien starters' finals

Working notes for Eien Blaziken, Eien Venusaur and Eien Greninja. Their concept, stats and
evolution moves are settled (`variants.md`, `brainstorm-eien-starters-evolved.md`); these are the
questions left before they're built. Decisions move into `variants.md` when settled; this file
stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- **The three finals** (`variants.md`, "Evolved stages"), each evolving at Lv 36 and keeping its
  line's Eien typing:
  - **Eien Blaziken (Fire/Ghost):** the Nebuta warrior float itself, glowing from inside, with a
    painted face; a towering fighter. 80 / 105 / 70 / 120 / 70 / 85. Learns Shadow Ball on
    evolving.
  - **Eien Venusaur (Grass/Psychic):** the flower fully open, the aurora streaming up from it
    into the sky. 80 / 73 / 83 / 110 / 105 / 74. Learns Psychic on evolving.
  - **Eien Greninja (Water/Ice):** a lone mountain guardian of the snow passes.
    72 / 95 / 67 / 103 / 81 / 112. Learns Freeze-Dry on evolving.
- **Abilities and moves:**
  - Abilities: Blaze / Cursed Body, Overgrow / Forewarn, Torrent / Snow Cloak.
  - Learnsets: the first stages' new moves, otherwise vanilla.
  - Shinies: night lantern, twilight, midnight.
- **Names:** "Eien" plus the vanilla name for now. A final form may get its own name if the
  design earns one.
- **Parked:** a signature move per final.
- **Art:** front-only concept drafts exist (`art/eien_evolved_contact_sheet.png`).
  - Venusaur's (an aurora streaming up from the flower) and Greninja's (a straw hat and cape)
    read clearly.
  - Blaziken's read as a ghost-flame fighter, not yet as a paper lantern float with a painted
    face.
- **Story:**
  - Akira's "evolved starter" is first seen winning his tournament semi-final (Act 2, the
    tournament, about Lv 34).
  - He battles the hero at about Lv 36 on Route 5 (Act 3).
  - The hero recognises Torchic's and Bulbasaur's lines but not Froakie's.
- **Precedent:** the middle stages are built with full art (fronts, idle frames, backs, icons,
  shinies), and the middle stages don't evolve yet.
