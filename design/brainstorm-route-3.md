# Brainstorm: the road east from Yunotaki (Route 3, placeholder name)

Working notes for the route east of Yunotaki, after gym 2. Decisions move into a new location
doc, `story-outline.md`, `progression.md` and `side-quests.md` when settled; this file stays as
the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- Yunotaki's east exit (Lavaridge's original exit) is blocked: "The road east is shut for the
  thaw." Where it leads is `TODO(design)`. Routes are numbered, landmarks named.
- Act 1 after gym 2: gyms 3 and 4, then the tournament (Act 2). He battles Nami between gyms 3
  and 4. Yuki's cameo is "a route after gym 2": she beats a trainer he struggled with, battles
  him briefly, says she'll see him at the tournament (starts her chain). Her hometown is
  `TODO(design)`.
- The region: northern Honshu's Sea of Japan coast (Tohoku, Aomori): fishing towns, snowy
  inland towns, old shrines, a volcanic mountain (the door mountain). Gyms 3-8 are
  `TODO(design)`. Levels after gym 2 (Medicham Lv 21) aren't set.
- The fading shows in small disasters (Route 2's thinning patch); the Eien Survey helps at them.
- Each gym so far had a catchable Eien variant as its answer nearby (Poochyena, Hoothoot).

## Decided

- **Destination (r1 1a):** down to the coast: a Sea of Japan fishing port (winter waves, wooden
  boats, drying racks) is the gym 3 town. Nami's Lapras belongs to this sea.
- **The thaw block (r1 2c):** the road stays washed out; the Eien Survey has made a way round.
  Helpful and organised, a quiet second sighting.
- **Story beats (r1 3c):** Yuki's cameo, plus a first sight of the door mountain smoking on the
  horizon from a ridge. Otherwise quiet.
- **Shape (r1 4b):** a river gorge with rope bridges and a short cave where the thaw broke the
  road (two maps); the cave fits the Survey's way round.
- **Variant (r1 5a):** a new Eien variant on this route as gym 3's answer, like Poochyena and
  Hoothoot.
- **Yuki (r1 6a):** midway, she beats a trainer who beat or nearly beat him, then battles him
  briefly.
- **Gym 3 type (r2 1b):** Electric: the Sea of Japan coast's winter thunderstorms that come with
  the first snow (yuki-okoshi, "snow-waking thunder"). The variant's job is a Ground answer.
- **The Survey's way round (r2 2a):** an old mine tunnel they cleared and hung lamps in; a member
  at the entrance ("It's safe. We checked."); a sign: "Route kept open by the Eien Survey."
  Hinata is saved for later.
- **The door mountain (r2 3b):** seen from the cave's exit, the first view after the dark.
- **Yuki (r2 4a):** a tough trainer blocks a rope bridge, a few levels above the curve; he
  battles them (win or lose), then Yuki arrives, beats them easily, and battles him briefly.
- **Variant family (r2 5a):** Wooper (to Quagsire): a gorge mud-dweller, already Ground. Its
  design gets its own brainstorm, like Hoothoot's.
- **Levels (r2 6a):** wild Lv 15-20, trainers 18-23, the bridge trainer about 25.
