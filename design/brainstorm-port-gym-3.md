# Brainstorm: the port town and gym 3

Working notes for the gym 3 town at the end of Route 3 and its gym. Decisions move into a new
location doc, `characters.md`, `progression.md` and `story-outline.md` when settled; this file
stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- A Sea of Japan fishing port (winter waves, wooden boats, drying racks) at the foot of Route 3's
  gorge; the Eien Survey's cave opens above it, with the first view of the coast and the door
  mountain. Gym 3 is Electric: the coast's winter thunderstorms that come with the first snow
  (yuki-okoshi, "snow-waking thunder").
- Eien Wooper (Ground, from Route 3) is the planned answer; its design isn't done yet.
- Akira's evolved starter shows at gym 3 (the evolved Eien starters are designed: Eien
  Combusken, Ivysaur, Frogadier at Lv 18).
- Nami: he battles her between gyms 3 and 4; an optional warm scene "the coast with her Lapras,
  after they battle".
- Levels: Route 3 trainers 18-23 (the bridge Ace Trainer about 25). Gym 2's ace was Lv 21.
- Names follow Japanese naming; towns are short poetic compounds (Hamakaze, Shimotsuki,
  Yunotaki). Names in use: Akira, Haru, Nami, Kaede, Fuyumi, Kashiwagi, Makoto, Chiyo, Yuki,
  Tetsu, Ren, Seiji, Minato, Hashimoto, Hinata, Ibuki, Daichi, Shizuka, Tomo, Saya.
- Gym pattern so far: a vanilla gym layout reused (Rustboro, Dewford), the leader's team carries
  one move against each answer, a badge with a local name, a TM.

## Decided

- **Town name (r1 1a):** Isaribi (漁火, "fishing fires"): the lamps of the night squid boats strung
  across the dark sea.
- **Leader (r1 2c):** the woman who runs the squid-boat fleet, whose lamps her Electric Pokémon
  light. Practical, loud, well loved; the first leader with a big, warm personality.
- **Team theme (r1 3d):** a mix: Lanturn the ace (the angler's lamp), with a Magneton (the harbour
  cranes) and a Shinx-line Pokémon (the storm).
- **Gym inside (r1 4a):** Mauville's gym (the vanilla Electric gym) reused, its switches and
  barriers read as lamps switching on.
- **Nami's battle (r1 5a):** on the port's pier after gym 3, her Lapras in the water behind her;
  the optional warm scene follows there on the coast.
- **Akira (r1 6b):** no battle; he's in the gym's stands watching and says one line afterwards
  about the tournament.
- **Leader's name (r2 1a):** Akari (灯, "lamp, light").
- **Team (r2 2a):** three: Magneton Lv 24 with Flash Cannon (Steel, against Rock), Luxio Lv 24
  with Ice Fang (against Ground and Grass), and the ace Lanturn Lv 27 with its Water moves
  (against Ground and Rock). One coverage move each, like gyms 1 and 2.
- **Badge and TM (r2 3b):** the Lantern Badge (after chōchin, 提灯, paper lanterns; it ties back
  to the Torchic line) and TM34 Shock Wave.
- **Gym trainers (r2 4a):** two of her fleet crew, a squid-boat fisher and a deckhand, with
  Chinchou, Mareep and Pikachu between them.
- **The town (r2 5b+c):** a harbour market with drying racks, the squid-boat pier, a lighthouse
  (not enterable); the fleet's boathouse, where her crew talk about her; a fishing-gear shop
  that sells the Good Rod (Spheal, held back at Lake Usurai, becomes catchable). Nami's pier at
  the end.
- **Weather and time (r2 6c):** a snow-and-thunder storm by day; the squid boats lit at night,
  the view from the pier (and Nami's warm scene).
