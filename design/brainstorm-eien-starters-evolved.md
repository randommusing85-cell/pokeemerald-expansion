Working notes for the starters' second and third stages (`variants.md`). Decisions move into
`variants.md` when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- First stages built: Eien Torchic (Fire/Ghost, a shrine-lantern bird), Eien Bulbasaur
  (Grass/Psychic, a fading bloom with a translucent bulb glowing like the aurora), Eien
  Froakie (Water/Ice, a snow ninja adapted to the cold; the one starter he doesn't
  recognise).
- Rules (`brainstorm-eien-species.md`): regional forms; the same stat total as the original;
  existing abilities, starters keep their pinch ability as the main one; starters evolve by
  level and keep their Eien typing; first evolution at about Lv 18 (not 16). A final form may
  get its own name. A signature move per starter's final stage is parked.
- The cycle holds: Torchic beats Bulbasaur, Bulbasaur beats Froakie, Froakie beats Torchic.
- Needed soon: Akira's starter is unevolved at gym 2 (Lv 17); the evolved form shows at gym
  3. Sprites go through AI drafts (`tools/eien_species/`, `art/eien_starters_drafts.md`).
