# Brainstorm: unique game mechanics

Running notes for the mechanics brainstorm. Nothing here is decided until it's under
**Decided**; decisions move into the real docs (game bible, progression) when the user agrees.

## Givens (from the existing design docs)

- Isekai story: a fan from our world, a fading god (Arceus), Palkia as the door between
  worlds, Celebi as a guide who can't battle. Romance, betrayal, on-screen deaths.
- Pokémon are innately good and loyal; the player's party never dies.
- The **journal** is a key item from Act 1; its pages go blank near the end.
- Eien regional variants (touched by the fading, or adapted to the cold); rare shinies.
- Friends (Haru, Yuki, Kaede, Tetsu, Ren) have their own battles; tag battles are open.
- Snow country: long winters, aurora skies, a volcanic "thin place".
- Engine: pokeemerald-expansion. Built-in switches exist for followers, DexNav, level caps,
  affection, day/night, inverse and sky battles, Mega/Z/Dynamax/Tera.
