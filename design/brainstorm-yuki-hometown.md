## Givens (from the docs)

- **Yuki** (幸, "good fortune"): cheerful and teasing, serious in battle; competes for her
  hometown, a small struggling place (a tournament win would bring money and attention); no
  sponsor. Her ace is Absol, the disaster Pokémon (in the games it appears before calamities).
  Look: farm girl on the road, a festival cord in her hair from her town's one festival; the cord
  is her keepsake at the goodbye.
- **The town:** a farming village a short route beyond Kanaya (gym 5), between gyms 5 and 6;
  leaning rice terraces and one festival a year.
- **Her step 2** (`side-quests.md`, E6): after gym 5 her letter asks for help; one of the fading's
  small disasters hits her town. Helping is how they become friends. Points from helping and an
  answer. Keep it friendship, no flirting.
- **The fading so far:** thinnings drain colour, stop the snow, make Pokémon flee (Route 2); an
  orchard blossoms and withers in an hour (Route 4); Tokoyo opened one on purpose (the
  tournament); greyed tiles in Act 3; Tokoyo now openly ropes thinnings off. Celebi flickers near
  them.
- **Other disaster quests:** E4 Hamakaze's harbour freezes early (Act 1); E5 a child goes missing
  near a thin place (Act 2 or 3). Kaede's step 2 already has a wild double battle beside her.
- **Levels:** after gym 5 (ace Lv 38); about Lv 36-40 (default).
