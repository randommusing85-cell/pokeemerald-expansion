## Givens (from the docs)

- **Yuki** (幸, "good fortune"): cheerful and teasing, serious in battle; competes for her
  hometown, a small struggling place (a tournament win would bring money and attention); no
  sponsor. Her ace is Absol, the disaster Pokémon (in the games it appears before calamities).
  Look: farm girl on the road, a festival cord in her hair from her town's one festival; the cord
  is her keepsake at the goodbye.
- **The town:** a farming village a short route beyond Kanaya (gym 5), between gyms 5 and 6;
  leaning rice terraces and one festival a year.
- **Her step 2** (`side-quests.md`, E6): after gym 5 her letter asks for help; one of the fading's
  small disasters hits her town. Helping is how they become friends. Points from helping and an
  answer. Keep it friendship, no flirting.
- **The fading so far:** thinnings drain colour, stop the snow, make Pokémon flee (Route 2); an
  orchard blossoms and withers in an hour (Route 4); Tokoyo opened one on purpose (the
  tournament); greyed tiles in Act 3; Tokoyo now openly ropes thinnings off. Celebi flickers near
  them.
- **Other disaster quests:** E4 Hamakaze's harbour freezes early (Act 1); E5 a child goes missing
  near a thin place (Act 2 or 3). Kaede's step 2 already has a wild double battle beside her.
- **Levels:** after gym 5 (ace Lv 38); about Lv 36-40 (default).

## Decided

- **The disaster (r1 1d):** on festival night a thinning opens at the rice terraces, and the
  village's Pokémon walk into it, grey, as if called.
- **Why she wrote (r1 2a):** Absol sensed it coming; her letter comes before it happens, and he
  arrives on festival day, in time. A calm scene with her family first.
- **What he does (r1 3c):** he and Yuki go into the grey to find the village's Pokémon (four or
  five, a wild battle each to calm them) and bring them back; at the centre, two Tokoyo members
  who came to measure the thinning rather than close it stand in the way of the last one: a tag
  battle beside Yuki.
- **The outcome (r1 4d):** the Pokémon come back and the festival finishes late that night, but
  the terraces stay grey for the year. Her "next year" line carries both.
- **Her family (r1 5a):** her parents farm the terraces; a little brother who wants to be a
  trainer like her.
- **The village (r1 6a):** Minori (実り, "harvest, ripening").
- **The festival (r2 1c):** *mushi-okuri*, insect-sending: torches carried along the paddies at
  night to drive pests away (a real Tohoku rite). The torch-bearers wear cords: Yuki's festival
  cord is one. The thinning opens mid-procession.
- **Her question (r2 2c):** "Will you come to the festival next year?" (yes / I'll try / I don't
  know). Light on the surface; the player knows he goes home. The answer gives the points.
- **Celebi and Absol (r2 3c):** Celebi flickers and waits at the grey's edge; Yuki's Absol leads,
  sensing where each Pokémon is.
- **The Tokoyo pair (r2 4a):** two ordinary members, new faces: they'll close it after taking
  readings. Calm and sure.
- **The road (r2 5a):** a short route (placeholder Route 6) of terraced hillside with a few
  trainers, from Kanaya to Minori.
- **Her brother (r2 6a):** gets his first Pokémon in the aftermath: one of the saved Pokémon
  chooses to stay with him.
