# Brainstorm: Act 3's opening and gym 5

Working notes. Decisions move into `story-outline.md`, `progression.md`, `characters.md`,
`side-quests.md` and new location docs when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- **Act 2 ends** in Shimotsuki: Fuyumi's funeral (Kaede keeps the rite), then the League's
  statement, "an accident". Akira hears it. He was named the tournament's winner; it felt hollow.
- **Act 3 ("The split", gyms 5-8), in order:** a few towns after the statement, Akira joins
  Minato openly; Yuki becomes a friend (her hometown's disaster, side-quests E6 / her step 2);
  he learns about the cycle and Minato's plan (drain other worlds, his own included); Nami tells
  him she chooses her world; the professor dies in a lab disaster (Hamakaze); he learns why
  Palkia brought him and where Celebi came from.
- **Act 3 side steps already placed:** Akira step 3 (an optional meeting after he leaves); Ren
  step 1 (uneasy about the League's story, quits either way); Kaede step 2 (the first aurora
  night at her shrine) and step 3 (before the League); Haru step 3 (after his mother dies);
  Mamoru turns up once; E3 aurora research; E5 a missing child near a thin place.
- **Fuyumi's promise** (look out for Akira) comes back when he leaves.
- **Map so far:** Hamakaze (coast) → Route 1 → Shimotsuki (snowy inland) → Lake Usurai → Route 2
  (mountain pass) → Yunotaki → Route 3 (gorge to the Sea of Japan coast) → Isaribi → Route 4
  (rice plain, inland) → Hanashiro (castle town). The door mountain (Osorezan) is seen from Route
  3; Tetsu, the old guide, lives near it.
- **Types used:** Ice, Fighting, Electric, Grass. Levels: the tournament ~30-34; gym 5 ~35-38
  (default).
- **Letters** replace Match Call; there's no fast travel decided yet.

## Decided

- **Where the road picks up (r1 1a):** from Shimotsuki, a new way out (east or north, not taken
  yet), toward the door mountain's side of the region. Shimotsuki becomes the game's hinge.
- **Gym 5 (r1 2a):** Fire, in an ironworks town like Morioka's Nanbu ironware: forges, kettles,
  an old mine. Ties to the Survey's "old seams" thread and Eien Quagsire's *tetsubin* look. The
  *itako* are saved for the door mountain.
- **Akira's turn (r1 3c):** on a route before gym 5, he waits for the hero to say goodbye
  properly and asks him not to follow. A battle. Whether the hero promised Fuyumi to look out
  for him decides one line.
- **Yuki's hometown (r1 4a):** right after gym 5, her letter asks for help.
- **Act 3's world (r1 5c):** both, lightly: a few greyed tiles on routes and more thinnings
  Tokoyo now openly ropes off; one League official per town repeating the "accident" story.
- **Gym 5's leader (r1 6a):** a League loyalist who repeats the "accident" line and believes it.
  Decent, not a villain; a contrast with Ibuki's doubt.
- **Getting to Shimotsuki (r2 1d):** the funeral scene takes him there (a fade); fast travel is
  decided separately, with field moves.
- **Route 5 (r2 2a):** east from Shimotsuki through old snowy beech forest (like
  Shirakami-Sanchi), quiet, with greyed patches in Act 3. Akira waits at its end.
- **Gym 5's leader (r2 3b):** the young heir of a foundry family, loyal to the League because the
  League made his family's name. Decent while being wrong.
- **His team (r2 4c):** Magcargo, Bronzong (a cast-metal bell from the forge), and a Coalossal ace
  (Rock/Fire, from the mine). Lv 35-38.
- **The gym (r2 5c):** Lavaridge's gym layout (its sand holes read as steam vents); the Forge
  Badge and TM35 Flamethrower.
- **Akira's goodbye battle (r2 6a):** his full team at about Lv 36, his evolved starter the ace.
