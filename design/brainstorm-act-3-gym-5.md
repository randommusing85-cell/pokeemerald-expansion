## Givens (from the docs)

- **Act 2 ends** in Shimotsuki: Fuyumi's funeral (Kaede keeps the rite), then the League's
  statement, "an accident". Akira hears it. He was named the tournament's winner; it felt hollow.
- **Act 3 ("The split", gyms 5-8), in order:** a few towns after the statement, Akira joins
  Minato openly; Yuki becomes a friend (her hometown's disaster, side-quests E6 / her step 2);
  he learns about the cycle and Minato's plan (drain other worlds, his own included); Nami tells
  him she chooses her world; the professor dies in a lab disaster (Hamakaze); he learns why
  Palkia brought him and where Celebi came from.
- **Act 3 side steps already placed:** Akira step 3 (an optional meeting after he leaves); Ren
  step 1 (uneasy about the League's story, quits either way); Kaede step 2 (the first aurora
  night at her shrine) and step 3 (before the League); Haru step 3 (after his mother dies);
  Mamoru turns up once; E3 aurora research; E5 a missing child near a thin place.
- **Fuyumi's promise** (look out for Akira) comes back when he leaves.
- **Map so far:** Hamakaze (coast) → Route 1 → Shimotsuki (snowy inland) → Lake Usurai → Route 2
  (mountain pass) → Yunotaki → Route 3 (gorge to the Sea of Japan coast) → Isaribi → Route 4
  (rice plain, inland) → Hanashiro (castle town). The door mountain (Osorezan) is seen from Route
  3; Tetsu, the old guide, lives near it.
- **Types used:** Ice, Fighting, Electric, Grass. Levels: the tournament ~30-34; gym 5 ~35-38
  (default).
- **Letters** replace Match Call; there's no fast travel decided yet.
