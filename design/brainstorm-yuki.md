## Givens (from the docs)

- **Who:** a girl from another town, a tournament competitor. A rival in Act 2, a main friend
  from Act 3, after Akira leaves. Keep it friendship: rivals who respect each other, no
  flirting (`side-quests.md`). She survives; he says goodbye to her on-screen. She fights
  beside him (with Seiji and Haru) against Minato and Akira at the door mountain.
- **Her chain (`side-quests.md`):** step 0, the Act 1 cameo on Route 3's rope bridge: KAITO
  (League-sponsored Ace Trainer: Kirlia 24, Staravia 24, Machoke 25) beats or nearly beats him;
  she beats KAITO easily, battles him briefly, "see you at the tournament". Step 1, a spar at
  the tournament. Step 2, Act 3: one of the fading's small disasters hits her hometown (E6);
  helping is how they become friends. Keepsake: `TODO(design)` a tournament token.
- `route-3.md` already reads her as unsponsored, against KAITO's League money.
- **Open in the docs:** her hometown, sprite, team, lines.
- **The cast's voices so far:** Kaede blunt and practical; Ren by-the-book and terse
  ("Next."); Akari loud and warm; Ibuki quiet. Signature Pokémon: Nami's Lapras, Kaede's
  Ghosts (Froslass, Mismagius).
- **Levels:** Route 3's cameo sits around Lv 24-25 (KAITO's); her team must look clearly
  stronger there, but the "brief" battle with him has to be fair for a Lv ~20 team.
