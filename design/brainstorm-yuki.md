# Brainstorm: Yuki's design

Working notes. Decisions move into `characters.md`, `side-quests.md`, `dialogue-style.md` and
`locations/route-3.md` when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- **Who:** a girl from another town, a tournament competitor. A rival in Act 2, a main friend
  from Act 3, after Akira leaves. Keep it friendship: rivals who respect each other, no
  flirting (`side-quests.md`). She survives; he says goodbye to her on-screen. She fights
  beside him (with Seiji and Haru) against Minato and Akira at the door mountain.
- **Her chain (`side-quests.md`):** step 0, the Act 1 cameo on Route 3's rope bridge: KAITO
  (League-sponsored Ace Trainer: Kirlia 24, Staravia 24, Machoke 25) beats or nearly beats him;
  she beats KAITO easily, battles him briefly, "see you at the tournament". Step 1, a spar at
  the tournament. Step 2, Act 3: one of the fading's small disasters hits her hometown (E6);
  helping is how they become friends. Keepsake: `TODO(design)` a tournament token.
- `route-3.md` already reads her as unsponsored, against KAITO's League money.
- **Open in the docs:** her hometown, sprite, team, lines.
- **The cast's voices so far:** Kaede blunt and practical; Ren by-the-book and terse
  ("Next."); Akari loud and warm; Ibuki quiet. Signature Pokémon: Nami's Lapras, Kaede's
  Ghosts (Froslass, Mismagius).
- **Levels:** Route 3's cameo sits around Lv 24-25 (KAITO's); her team must look clearly
  stronger there, but the "brief" battle with him has to be fair for a Lv ~20 team.

## Decided

- **What drives her (r1 1a):** she competes for her hometown, a small struggling place; a
  tournament win would bring it money and attention. Unsponsored. Act 3's disaster hits what
  she's fighting for.
- **Team shape (r1 2b):** mixed, with one clear ace she's had since she was small.
- **Her ace (r1 3a):** Absol, the disaster Pokémon (in the games it appears before calamities).
  Ties her to the fading's disasters and her hometown's. Not Ice.
- **Her name (r1 4b):** 幸, "happiness, good fortune": a warm counterweight to the disasters,
  quietly ironic with Absol.
- **Hometown (r1 5d):** left for Act 3's map planning; leaning a small farming village (rice
  terraces, one festival a year). `TODO(design)`.
- **Voice (r1 6a):** cheerful and teasing; ribs him, laughs off a loss, serious in battle.
- **The bridge battle (r2 1d):** Absol alone, Lv 22 (fair for his ~Lv 20 team), plus a line
  showing she held back (her others are tired from KAITO). Win or lose, the scene goes on.
- **KAITO's loss (r2 2b):** a short scripted scene, not a battle: her Absol moves, KAITO's
  Machoke falls, an emote and two text boxes. KAITO walks off.
- **Her look (r2 3d):** a farm girl on the road: straw hat on her back, a work jacket, walking
  boots, a cloth bag; and a festival cord in her hair from her town's one festival.
- **The keepsake (r2 4b):** the festival cord from her hair, given at the goodbye (replaces
  `side-quests.md`'s "tournament token").
- **A second Act 1 meeting (r2 5c):** a heal-and-chat at a Pokémon Center near gym 4: she
  teases him about the bridge. No battle. `TODO(design)`: which town (gym 4 isn't placed).
- **Her first line (r2 6b):** "You didn't lose by much. I'd have lost to him, last year."
  (Drafted for after KAITO; works win or lose with a variant.)
