## Givens (from the docs)

- **Region:** Eien, northern Honshu's Sea of Japan coast (Tohoku, Aomori): fishing towns, snowy
  inland towns, old shrines, and the volcanic door mountain (modeled on Osorezan), first seen
  smoking on the horizon from Route 3's cave exit. Map layout and route count are open.
- **Act 1 so far:** Hamakaze → Route 1 → Shimotsuki (gym 1, Ice) → Lake Usurai → Route 2 →
  Yunotaki (gym 2, Fighting) → Route 3 → Isaribi (gym 3, Electric, the Lantern Badge). Then
  "... → gym 4 → tournament" (Act 2, the midpoint). Act 1 is gyms 1-4.
- **Things that need a place:** Yuki's heal-and-chat at a Pokémon Center near gym 4 (her step
  0b). Isaribi's onward road (`isaribi.md`). Tetsu, the old mountain guide, lives near the door
  mountain. The tournament's location is `TODO(design)`.
- **Already in play:** the Eien Survey (helpful, organised; Hinata is saved for later); the
  "old seams" thread; Akira's evolved starter first shows at the tournament; Nami's warm scene
  is on Isaribi's pier.
- **Level curve:** gym 3's ace Lv 27. Gym 4 roughly 28-32 (default).
- **Maps:** new maps are made by the user in Porymap; vanilla layouts can be reused (Yunotaki
  reused Lavaridge, Isaribi's gym reuses Mauville's).

## Decided

- **The road (r1 1b):** inland from Isaribi across the rice plain to a castle town. A change
  from snow and sea.
- **Gym 4's town (r1 2a):** a castle town with apple orchards, like Hirosaki: castle walls,
  cherry trees, Aomori's apples.
- **Gym 4's type (r1 3a):** Grass. Fire, Ice, Flying, Poison and Bug answer it; the Froakie
  line and Water/Ground catches struggle; Eien Wooper (Ground/Steel) is neutral.
- **The road's beat (r1 4b+a):** a bigger thinning than Route 2's (a stretch of the plain goes
  grey for a while; the fading is getting worse). The Eien Survey ropes it off, and one member
  asks about Celebi a little too closely. Not Hinata.
- **The tournament (r1 5a):** in gym 4's town, in the castle grounds: Act 1 ends where Act 2
  begins, and the disaster that kills Fuyumi happens in a town the player knows.
- **Length (r1 6a):** one route (placeholder Route 4), then the castle town. Yuki's heal-and-
  chat is at the castle town's Pokémon Center.
- **Gym 4's leader (r2 1c):** the castle's head gardener (a man): keeps the cherry trees and moss
  gardens; quiet and exact; watches the trees for the fading.
- **At the tournament disaster (r2 2a):** he survives, shaken; helps with the aftermath and
  turns up once in Act 3.
- **His team (r2 3c):** Cherrim, Dipplin, and an Appletun ace (Grass/Dragon, so Ice answers it:
  a nod to Fuyumi). Lv 28-32 (default; the ace 32).
- **The gym (r2 4a):** Fortree's gym layout reused; its turnstiles read as orchard gates.
- **Badge and TM (r2 5a):** the Blossom Badge, TM19 Giga Drain.
- **Route 4's thinning (r2 6b):** an orchard blossoms and withers in an hour, out of season. The
  Survey ropes it off; one member asks about Celebi a little too closely. The gardener talks
  about it after the badge.
- **The castle town (r3 1a):** Hanashiro (花城, "flower castle").
- **The gardener (r3 2a):** Mamoru (守, "to protect"): the trees he keeps.
- **The route (r3 3a):** "ROUTE 4" on the region map; the routes get named together later.
- **The Survey member (r3 4b+c):** seen again at the tournament near where the villains move,
  and the one who reports Celebi to Minato (said or shown later). Name `TODO(design)`.
- **Gym 4's trainers (r3 5a):** two apprentice gardeners, Grass Pokémon, Lv 27-29.
- **Route 4's people (r3 6c):** locals in the fields (rice farmers, apple pickers, a scarecrow
  maker) and tournament-goers on the road (League hopefuls, fans; mixed teams).
