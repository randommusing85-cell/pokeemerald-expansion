# Tobira-yama (the door mountain)

- **Map(s):** made in Porymap (placeholder constants for the caldera lake path, the riverbed of
  souls, the slopes, the summit and the door).
- **Type:** dungeon (a thin place)
- **Connects to:** Tokiwa Hall ([tokiwa-hall.md](tokiwa-hall.md)) and Kawara ([kawara.md](kawara.md))
  across the caldera lake; Tetsu's hut is the gate to the climb.

Decided in [brainstorm-act-4.md](../brainstorm-act-4.md). Tobira-yama (扉山, "door mountain"): an old name whose meaning people have
forgotten. Modeled on Osorezan; seen smoking from Route 3; a thin place (`src/eien_places.c`, once the
maps exist).

## Purpose

The end: where Minato builds his machine to draw on Palkia's door, and where the cycle finishes.

## Story beats here

Act 4 beats 3-5 in [story-outline.md](../story-outline.md).

1. **The climb:** the caldera lake path → **Kaede's tag battle** beside him against two Tokoyo
   members at the riverbed of souls (her finale; [characters.md](../characters.md)) → **Tetsu's
   path and Ren** holding the side trail (if their points are high) → **Nami's last battle** near
   the summit, on Minato's side. Haru and Yuki go up with Seiji.
2. **The summit:** **Akira** one on one (his full team, Lv 61; with high points he steps aside
   afterwards). Then he and **Seiji against Minato** (Lv 63). Minato's machine fires to pull him
   through the door; **Celebi takes it and dies** (plain, physical, no speech). Minato walks away
   in silence. A small stack of stones marks the place afterwards (the riverbed's custom; the
   epilogue's "where Celebi died").
3. **Palkia at the door:** he walks into the light; the cycle finishes through him; the colour comes
   back (Arceus renewing, felt, not shown); his memories start to go. Palkia gives him until dawn.
4. **Dawn:** after the goodbye night ([story-outline.md](../story-outline.md), Act 4 beat 5) he
   steps into the door's light; the journal's pages go blank; Kaede sees the tale happen.
