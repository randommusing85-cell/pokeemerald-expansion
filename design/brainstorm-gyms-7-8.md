# Brainstorm: gyms 7 and 8 and the door mountain approach

Working notes. Decisions move into `story-outline.md`, `progression.md`, `characters.md`,
`side-quests.md` and new location docs when settled; this file stays as the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- **Where we are:** Act 3 after the professor's death. He has fast travel (Kumo, from the Town
  Map). He has learned the cycle, the cover-up and Tokoyo's plan (Hoshimi), why Palkia brought him
  and how a cycle can end safely (her notes). Akira has joined Minato; Nami chose her world; Yuki
  is a friend; Ren quit. Shimotsuki: Akira at Fuyumi's grave, Kaede's aurora night. Then gyms 7-8.
- **Act 4:** the Pokémon League (Minato's plan starts partway through the Elite Four; Seiji admits
  the cover-up and joins him), then the door mountain (Osorezan): Kaede's tag battle on the way up
  (Tetsu and Ren hold others off with enough points); with Seiji, Haru and Yuki he fights Minato
  and Akira; Celebi dies; the cycle finishes.
- **The door mountain** is volcanic, seen smoking from Route 3; the *itako* (spirit mediums) are
  saved for it. **Tetsu**, the old mountain guide, lives near it: step 1, climbers lost in the snow
  after a small slide (find them with him); step 2, his hut, the old paths, the climbing gear
  (shortcuts only). Kaede's step 3 is "before the League". E5 (a child missing near a thin place)
  is unplaced. Mamoru (gym 4) turns up once in Act 3.
- **Types used:** Ice, Fighting, Electric, Grass, Fire, Psychic. Leaders so far: Fuyumi, Ibuki,
  Akari, Mamoru, Takumi, Tsukiko. Gym 6's ace Lv 45; gyms 7-8 ~48-55 (default).
- **The League's location** isn't placed yet.

## Decided

- **Where (r1 1a):** both on the door mountain's peninsula (like Shimokita): a fishing port at the
  cape (gym 7) and the town at the mountain's foot (gym 8). The map narrows toward the end.
- **Types (r1 2a):** gym 7 Water, a tuna port on a rough strait (like Ōma); gym 8 Ghost, the temple
  town at the mountain's foot, led by an *itako* (Kaede uses Ghosts too, but isn't a leader).
- **The leaders (r1 3a):** gym 7's harbour master sides with Minato: saving this world is worth
  draining others; decent, sure, on the other side. Gym 8's *itako* is on no side and knows the
  old tales are true.
- **Tetsu (r1 4a):** between gyms 7 and 8: the slide and the lost climbers on the road to the
  mountain town, then his hut (the climbing gear's shortcuts help on the way to gym 8).
- **The League (r1 5a):** on the peninsula too, across the caldera from the door mountain: Act 4
  runs straight from the League to the mountain, and the League sits in sight of the door.
- **E5 (r1 6c):** back in Hanashiro: a child missing near a thin place; Mamoru's one Act 3
  appearance; fast travel put to use.
- **Gym 7's leader (r2 1a):** a grizzled woman who runs the tuna boats: blunt, proud of the port,
  quietly convinced. She lost her husband to the strait and won't lose her town to the fading.
- **Gym 7's team (r2 2a):** Sharpedo, Wailord and a Kingdra ace, Lv 48-50; Sootopolis's gym reused
  (its ice floor read as slick, icy decks).
- **Gym 7's badge (r2 3a):** the Tide Badge, TM03 Water Pulse.
- **Gym 8's leader (r2 4c):** an old blind *itako*, as they traditionally are: speaks for the dead at
  the mountain's festival; quiet, unhurried, kind; has "heard of him from the dead" (a hint only).
  She knew Kaede's grandmother Chiyo: an old tie between the two keepers of the tales.
- **Gym 8's team (r2 5a):** Banette (dolls left at the temple), Dusknoir, and a Chandelure ace
  (lanterns for the dead), Lv 52-55; Petalburg's gym reused (its rooms as the temple's halls).
- **Gym 8's badge (r2 6a):** the Bell Badge, TM30 Shadow Ball.
