# Brainstorm: the gym 2 leader (team and name)

Working notes for the hot-spring town's gym leader (`locations/onsen-town.md`). Decisions
move into `characters.md`, `progression.md` and the town doc when settled; this file stays as
the record.

## Givens (from the docs)

- Gym 2 is Fighting, in the hot-spring town. The leader is a former League fighter who left
  the League and trains under the hot waterfalls, like Tohoku's mountain ascetics; quietly
  sceptical of the League (a seed for the cover-up, not spelled out).
- Levels (defaults): gym trainers about Lv 17-19, the ace about Lv 21. Starters evolve at
  about Lv 18, before gym 2.
- Eien Hoothoot (Ghost/Flying, caught on the pass) is the intended answer: immune to Fighting
  moves. The gym should carry a Rock or Dark move so it isn't a free win.
- Gym 1's pattern: a small, bulky team where each Pokémon carries one move for the starters
  that resist its type (Fuyumi: Water Gun, Rock Tomb).
- The player's likely team at gym 2: an evolved Eien starter (Torchic line Fire/Ghost,
  immune to Fighting; Bulbasaur line Grass/Psychic, resists Fighting; Froakie line
  Water/Ice, weak to Fighting), Eien Poochyena or Mightyena (Rock/Dark: 4x weak to Fighting),
  Eien Hoothoot, Machop, Starly.
- Names follow Japanese naming. Names already used: Akira, Haru, Nami, Kaede, Fuyumi,
  Kashiwagi, Yuki, Tetsu, Ren, Seiji, Minato, Hashimoto (and the route trainers' placeholders).

## Decided

- **Who (r1 1a):** a man in his 40s who walked away from the League after a season that ended
  badly. He doesn't say why.
- **Name (r1 2b):** Ibuki (伊吹, "breath"; breath control is central to waterfall training).
- **Team size (r1 3b):** three: Lv 19, 19, 21.
- **Ace (r1 4c):** Medicham (Fighting/Psychic), the meditating ascetic; under its usual
  evolution level, as gym aces often are. Its Psychic weakness keeps the Hoothoot plan fair.
- **Coverage (r1 5a):** one coverage move per Pokémon, spread out: Rock Tomb on one (hits
  Hoothoot and the Torchic line), Bite on another, the ace's own Psychic on the third. No
  single Pokémon shuts down every answer.
- **Badge and TM (r1 6a + Brick Break):** the Misogi Badge (the waterfall purification rite)
  and TM31 Brick Break.

## Gaps and tensions

- Eien Poochyena / Mightyena, gym 1's answer, is 4x weak to Fighting: gym 2 punishes the
  team that gym 1 rewarded. That's a fair "new problem" if Hoothoot is easy to get.
- A Dark move (Bite, Knock Off) hits Eien Hoothoot, the Torchic line (Ghost) and the
  Bulbasaur line (Psychic) at once: one move could undo all three answers. Spread coverage
  thin.
- Most Fighting lines evolve after Lv 21 (Machoke 28, Hariyama 24, Medicham 37): an evolved
  ace at Lv 21 is under its usual level, as vanilla gym leaders often are.
- The badge's battle-sprite / overworld sprite for the leader: a new NPC sprite and trainer
  pic (AI drafts, like Fuyumi's).
