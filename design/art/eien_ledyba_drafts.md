# Eien Ledyba and Eien Ledian: AI draft front sprites

Front drafts for the two Bug/Fire forms in `variants.md` (ember fireflies of the summer rice
paddies: glowing ember spots, an abdomen lit like a coal; Eien Ledian's light brightened into a
lantern-like glow on its back). Nothing here is picked yet; the game uses the vanilla sprites in
placeholder ember palettes.

Contact sheets (vanilla first, then each converted draft, at 2x):
[`eien_ledyba_contact_sheet.png`](eien_ledyba_contact_sheet.png),
[`eien_ledian_contact_sheet.png`](eien_ledian_contact_sheet.png).

## How they were made

`tools/eien_species/variant_drafts.py`, as for Eien Wooper and Hoothoot: frame 1 of the vanilla
front enlarged 8x, the shared brief and one concept, on `gemini-3-pro-image` and
`gemini-3.1-flash-image`, one call each, no cherry-picking. The concepts: **a** a red-brown shell
with glowing ember spots, **b** a charcoal shell like burnt wood with glowing ember cracks, **c**
a warm amber shell with a real firefly's light.

## Eien Ledyba

| Draft | Concept | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini3pro_a` | a, ember spots | **Suggested.** A dark red shell with glowing orange spots and an abdomen lit like a coal: the clearest "ember firefly", still clearly Ledyba. |
| `gemini31flash_a` | a, ember spots | The same idea with a darker head; close to 3 Pro a. |
| `gemini3pro_b` | b, charcoal | Black shell with glowing lava cracks; dramatic, but it reads as molten rock more than a firefly. |
| `gemini31flash_b` | b, charcoal | Charcoal-red with ember rings for spots; busy at 1x. |
| `gemini3pro_c` | c, firefly light | An amber shell with a glowing firefly light at the abdomen's tip: the most firefly-like, but the shell is a dull brown. |
| `gemini31flash_c` | c, firefly light | Brighter amber and a soft yellow glow; warm but low in contrast. |
