# Drafts: Akira (battle)

## Battle sprite drafts

**Picked:** `akira_gemini31flash_b` (pointing), in the game as `TRAINER_PIC_EIEN_AKIRA`
(`graphics/trainers/front_pics/eien_akira.png`). `battle_ingame.png` is a debug battle with the
debug opponent's picture swapped for the test only.

Contact sheet: [../eien_akira_battle_contact_sheet.png](../eien_akira_battle_contact_sheet.png).
A trainer front picture (64x64) for his lab battle and later ones. Made by
`tools/eien_npcs/trainer_pic_drafts.py`: Gemini 3 Pro Image and 3.1 Flash Image got FRLG's
Youngster for style, proportions and framing, and his overworld sprite for colors and design
(spiky light brown hair, orange shirt, blue overalls). Each draft was sampled to 64x64 and cut
to its own 15 colors (`battle_drafts/`, raw output in `battle_drafts/raw/`). AI-assisted art,
to be noted in `asset-credits.md` when one is used.

- **a, Poké Ball held up:** Flash a is clean but small. Pro a drew him twice; unusable.
- **b, pointing:** Flash b is the clearest: right size, a big grin, his overworld colors.
  Pro b is tiny and thin.
- **c, fist pump:** Flash c has the most energy but came out blocky (drawn on a coarser grid).
  Pro c is squashed.
