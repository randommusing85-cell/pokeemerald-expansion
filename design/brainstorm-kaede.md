# Brainstorm: Kaede

Running notes. Nothing is decided until it's under **Decided**; decisions move into
`characters.md`, `side-quests.md`, `dialogue-style.md` and the Shimotsuki doc when the user
agrees.

## Givens

- A young shrine keeper who knows the old tales of the cycle (`characters.md`). The docs call
  her "she".
- Her shrine is in Shimotsuki, gym 1's town (Fuyumi, Akira's mother). She's met there after
  gym 1. It's a shrine area: Eien Poochyena can first evolve there (`variants.md`).
- The lore thread: shrine tablets and folk tales. The League and scientists hide the cycle;
  ordinary people only have folk tales (`game-bible.md`).
- A smaller friend with relationship points and a 3-step chain (`side-quests.md`):
  1. tablet rubbings (Spell Tag), 2. a folk tale "happening" on an aurora night at a thin
  place, 3. before the League, what the tales say about the end of a cycle: "someone always
  pays" (a charm item, `TODO(design)`).
- She always comes to help at the door mountain; smaller friends with points hold off part
  of Minato's forces in their own battles. Her gift is an item or TM tied to her.
- Celebi follows the hero on the map. Dialogue: short, plain, in character
  (`dialogue-style.md`).
