# Brainstorm: Kaede

Running notes. Nothing is decided until it's under **Decided**; decisions move into
`characters.md`, `side-quests.md`, `dialogue-style.md` and the Shimotsuki doc when the user
agrees.

## Givens

- A young shrine keeper who knows the old tales of the cycle (`characters.md`). The docs call
  her "she".
- Her shrine is in Shimotsuki, gym 1's town (Fuyumi, Akira's mother). She's met there after
  gym 1. It's a shrine area: Eien Poochyena can first evolve there (`variants.md`).
- The lore thread: shrine tablets and folk tales. The League and scientists hide the cycle;
  ordinary people only have folk tales (`game-bible.md`).
- A smaller friend with relationship points and a 3-step chain (`side-quests.md`):
  1. tablet rubbings (Spell Tag), 2. a folk tale "happening" on an aurora night at a thin
  place, 3. before the League, what the tales say about the end of a cycle: "someone always
  pays" (a charm item, `TODO(design)`).
- She always comes to help at the door mountain; smaller friends with points hold off part
  of Minato's forces in their own battles. Her gift is an item or TM tied to her.
- Celebi follows the hero on the map. Dialogue: short, plain, in character
  (`dialogue-style.md`).

## Decided

- **Role (1a):** she keeps the folk version. She tells the tales as tales and doesn't know
  they're true; he sees the truth behind them, sometimes before she does.
- **Voice (2c):** blunt and practical. Tired of being young and in charge, no patience for
  ceremony, but she does it all properly.
- **Shimotsuki ties (3c):** none close. She knows Fuyumi and Akira as neighbors; her story is
  her own.
- **Why she's young (4a):** an elderly keeper, her grandparent, still lives at the shrine but
  can't do the work. A second NPC there. (Placeholder: no name, gender or relation beyond
  "grandparent" yet.)
- **Her Pokémon (5a):** her partner is an Eien Mightyena, the shrine's guardian: it shows the
  player what Eien Poochyena becomes.
- **First meeting (6b+c):** she's reading her shrine's tablet when he arrives, asks if he read
  the one on Route 1, sees his Poochyena and says the stone dogs grow at their post.

- **First milestone (r2 1c):** a cameo: she's sweeping at the shrine and says one blunt line.
  The real first meeting is in the second milestone.
- **Age and look (r2 2a):** about his age, a teen. Shrine keeper's clothes worn practically:
  red hakama, a heavy winter coat over them, boots for the snow.
- **Team (r2 3a):** Eien Mightyena plus Ghost types (species `TODO(design)`).
- **He's not from here (r2 4b):** she notices and doesn't pry: one short remark.
- **"Someone always pays" (r2 5a):** she tells it flatly, as just a tale; he and the player
  make the connection.
- **The grandparent (r2 6a+b):** small talk and the old days; repeats the evolution rule if the
  player missed it; gives the charm item at quest step 3.
