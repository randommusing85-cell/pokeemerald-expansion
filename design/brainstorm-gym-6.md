## Decided

- **Gym 6 (r1 1a):** Psychic, at a League research station: an observatory where the League's
  scientists study the aurora and the fading. Where the truth is kept, and where beat 3 happens.
- **How he learns the truth (r1 2a):** Ren, who has just quit, gets him into the League's records
  (Ren's step 2). They read it together: the cycle and its cover-up from the League's side, and
  Tokoyo's plan as the League understands it.
- **Nami's choice (r1 3b):** on the road just after the reveal: she knew he'd find out and came to
  say it herself.
- **The professor (r1 4a):** Haru's letter calls him home (she's close to a breakthrough); a scene
  takes him to Hamakaze; the lab disaster happens while he's there.
- **Gym 6's leader (r1 5c):** the station's head scientist: hid the truth for years and now opens
  the records because the fading is speeding up. Someone who changes their mind (the villains
  never do).
- **Route 7 (r1 6a):** up into the highlands: a plateau of moor and wind, the station's domes on the
  skyline, the aurora visible by day.
- **The head scientist (r2 1a):** an older woman, an astronomer: precise and tired, decades of
  aurora readings. She hid it because panic would have killed more people than the fading.
- **Her team (r2 2a):** Solrock, Lunatone and a Xatu ace (in the games Xatu watches the sun and is
  said to see past and future). Lv 42-45.
- **The gym (r2 3a):** Mossdeep's gym layout (the vanilla Psychic gym: spin tiles, switches).
- **Badge and TM (r2 4a):** the Aurora Badge, TM29 Psychic.
- **The order (r2 5b):** records first: Ren gets him into the archive before the gym; he challenges
  her knowing what she hid, and she answers for it afterwards.
- **Beat 6 (r2 6d):** after the lab disaster, the professor's last research (found with Haru, his
  step 3) says plainly why Palkia brought him; Celebi shows him a glimpse of the ended world it came
  from.
- **The station (r3 1a):** Hoshimi (星見, "star-viewing").
- **The astronomer (r3 2a):** Tsukiko (月子, "moon child").
- **The route (r3 3a):** "ROUTE 7" on the region map; the routes get named together later.
- **Kaede's aurora night (r3 4b):** after the lab, on the way back east: grieving, he stops at her
  shrine in Shimotsuki; her flat telling of the tale about cycles lands after the professor's death.
- **Back east (r3 5a):** fast travel unlocks after Hamakaze, given by Haru (a rail pass, or a
  Pokémon that can now Fly him). Details: a fast-travel brainstorm.
- **Akira's optional meeting (r3 6b):** in Shimotsuki, at his mother's grave, on the trip home.
