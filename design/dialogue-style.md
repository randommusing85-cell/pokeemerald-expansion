# Dialogue style

## Rules for all dialogue

- Short lines, plain words. People talk; they don't narrate.
- One idea per text box. Split long speeches, cut most of them.
- No overdramatic lines, monologues about destiny, or "AI voice".
- Characters sound different from each other (see below).
- The game's text has no em/en dashes; use `-`, `…` or two sentences.

## Voices

For each major character, a line of description and 2–3 example lines in their voice.
