# Dialogue style

## Rules for all dialogue

- Short lines, plain words. People talk; they don't narrate.
- One idea per text box. Split long speeches, cut most of them.
- No overdramatic lines, monologues about destiny, or "AI voice".
- Characters sound different from each other (see below).
- The game's text has no em/en dashes; use `-`, `…` or two sentences.

## Story-wide rules

- **The hero** is mostly silent and speaks only in big scenes. His inner thoughts show his
  game knowledge ("In the games, this is where..."), and sometimes that knowledge is wrong.
- **Villains** believe they're right. In defeat they're gracious, never sore or gloating,
  and they don't change their minds.
- **Deaths** are shown plainly and briefly. No speeches at the deathbed.

## Voices

For each major character, a line of description and 2–3 example lines in their voice.

### Kaede

Blunt and practical; short sentences, no ceremony. Tells old tales flatly, as tales.

> You read the one on Route 1? Good. Nobody reads them.
> It'll grow when it's ready. Here, not out there. That's how the stone dogs are.
> You don't know the tales. Everyone here knows the tales. …Anyway.

### Yuki

Cheerful and teasing; ribs him, laughs off a loss, serious in battle. Drafts:

> You didn't lose by much. I'd have lost to him, last year.
> (`TODO(design)`: a variant for when he beat KAITO.)

### Haru

Eager and friendly; talks a little fast, teases without edge, and wants to be useful.
Loves his map.

> I'm drawing a map of Eien. For you. You don't know where anything is yet.
> Thanks! Don't get lost. It's one road.

### Akira

Cheerful, quick to grin, sure of himself without meanness; talks about his mum's gym and the
League. Loses lightly.

> New kid! I'm Akira. You pick first. I'll win with any of them.
> Aw. You're good.
> Come find me. I'll show you the gym. Mum runs it.

### Professor Kashiwagi

Warm and quick, a note-taker; questions come out like observations, and she doesn't fuss.

> You're half frozen. Come in. Sit by the stove.
> Write things down while they're fresh. I always do.

### Makoto

Few words, all practical; he feeds people before he asks them anything.

> The beach, in this? Where have you come from?
> …All right. Eat first.
> Back again? Sit. I'll see to them.
