# Brainstorm: story and narrative

Working notes from brainstorming sessions (see `.claude/skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md`).
Decisions move into the game bible, story outline and character docs once they're firm.

## Decided

- **Protagonist:** a guy from our world who is isekai'd into the Pokémon world.
- **Romance:** the story has one.
- **Structure:** gyms, the Elite Four, a tournament arc, and an overarching good vs. evil plot.
- **Dark beats:** a betrayal, loss, and deaths of NPCs.
- **Pokémon are innately good and loyal** to their trainers. Morality and viewpoints belong
  to the trainers, not the Pokémon.
- **Villains don't see themselves as evil.** They have a coherent point of view, won't
  change their minds, and lose gracefully (not sore losers).
- **Arrival:** a legendary pulled him into this world on purpose. He learns why late in the game.
- **He's a Pokémon fan** from our world. He knows type matchups and the games, and the story
  plays on where the real world differs from the games he knows.
- **Villains' belief:** other worlds exist (including his), and they want to open a door to
  them. His existence proves them right.
- **Romance:** with someone in the villains' group. The romance survives the story.
- **Betrayal:** the rival comes to agree with the villains and joins them openly.
- **Deaths:** mostly side effects of the villains' plan (disasters, collapses), not murders,
  plus a few major deaths shown on-screen.
- **Pokémon can die.** Major characters' Pokémon die on-screen, showing sacrifice and
  loyalty; minor NPCs' Pokémon die off-screen.
- **Why the villains want the door:** this world is dying because Arceus is fading. Other
  worlds are the way out. (Why Arceus is fading is still open.)
- **The legendary is Palkia, and Palkia is the door.** It brought him as a test: will someone
  from outside choose to keep the worlds apart or join them? The villains are half right.
- **Love interest: Nami** (she), a committed believer in the villains' cause who never changes
  her mind. He learns she's with them midway through. They choose each other while still
  disagreeing.
- **Rival's betrayal:** after a death the League failed to prevent, the rival decides the
  "good" side can't protect anyone.
- **On-screen deaths:** the mentor/professor figure, a gym leader, and a villain the player
  respects (possibly Nami's mentor).
- **Player's Pokémon:** his party is never killed. A story Pokémon he travels with dies in a
  scripted scene.
- **Protagonist voice:** mostly silent, speaks in big scenes. Fixed male lead.

## Gaps and tensions

- **If the world really is dying, the villains are right about the problem.** The heroes
  need an answer besides "don't open the door", or the good side just lets everyone die.
  The ending has to supply a third path, or a real cost.
- **Palkia and Arceus:** does Palkia's test serve Arceus, or go against it?
- **Why Palkia chose *him*** out of everyone in our world.
- **What "the door" physically is:** a place, a ritual, a machine, Palkia itself.
- **Does the public know Arceus is fading?** Changes how the League and ordinary people act.
- **Two of his closest people end up on the villains' side** (love interest and rival).
  Strong, but the good side needs its own anchors (mentor, friends, gym leaders).
- **Ending: stay or go home** is now central: the door the villains want could also send
  him home, and the love interest is tied to it.
- **Fan knowledge in gameplay:** how does "he knows the games" show up for the player?
- **Engine work for the lead:** remove Emerald's boy/girl choice and give him lines in big
  scenes (small script change, noted for later).
- **Loyal Pokémon on the villains' side.** Their Pokémon love them too. This could be a
  theme ("loyalty doesn't mean they're right") or a problem if the villains' goal harms
  Pokémon. Pick which.
- **Tournament placement:** before the League, after it, or instead of part of it.
- **Scope.** 8 gyms + Elite Four + tournament + villain plot is a full-size game. The
  first milestone (`design/README.md`) should be a small slice of it.
- **Region, name, tone** are all still blank in the game bible.
